How I work

I don’t start with the course. I start with the problem.

Sometimes the answer is a course. Sometimes it’s a video, a workshop, a badging exam, in-product guidance, or several of these working together.

My approach to learning strategy →

  • What are people trying to do?
  • What is getting in their way?
  • What do they need to know?
  • Where will they look for it?
  • What does the business need to happen next?

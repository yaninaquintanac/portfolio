Appian’s first AI learning portfolio

Turning a brand-new technology into practical learning for every audience.

The challenge

Generative AI was new to Appian’s developers, customers, partners, and internal teams, and there was no learning to build on.

My role

Program creator.

Built Appian’s early AI learning programs from the ground up.

The work

An AI video playlist curated with Customer Success from real customer questions, plus introductory AI courses for Partner Sales accreditation. I also introduced Amazon Kiro to streamline go-to-market and YouTube workflows.

The outcome

A foundation for AI learning across four audiences, including content that supports partner sales accreditation.

Appian Essentials

Ongoing strategy for the company’s most-trafficked developer learning course.

The challenge

The front door for new developers had to stay accurate and relevant while the platform kept changing.

My role

Content owner.

Owned the course’s strategy end to end. Rescripted it every year for the newest capabilities and introduced in-house casting: recruited and coached Appian talent, then produced each video refresh with the media team.

The work

Regularly audited and updated the content, and shaped it around the questions audiences actually ask.

The outcome

Videos from the course were used to onboard Appian executives, customers, and developers, and account executives adopted them for customer communications. The main video drew more than 52,000 views in a single year.

Appian Community YouTube

A content formula and SEO playbook that grew a developer channel.

The challenge

A new developer channel needed content people would find, watch, and come back to.

My role

Core launch team member, then growth lead.

Led the channel’s content-growth strategy.

The work

Developed the content formula and proved it with the channel’s highest-performing videos, then created an SEO procedure and trained the team on scriptwriting and SEO.

The outcome

Views and watch time grew about 300% in 2023 and 200% in 2024.

Marketing learning like a product

Getting good learning in front of the people who need it.
